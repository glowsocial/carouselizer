Questions.

What is a carousel?

A carousel is one post made of several slides that people swipe through. Boomp makes eight distinct slides. The first opens one idea and the last gives your business's next step.

Is it really free?

Yes. One business can make one complete eight-slide carousel free. The free tool does not start a subscription.

Do I need an account or a card?

No. You need an email, so we can send your carousel and a private link to save it.

How long does it take?

Several minutes. Boomp writes and designs every slide, then renders each one and the PDF. We email the result with a private link.

Where can I post it?

On Instagram, upload the eight slide images in order. On LinkedIn, use the PDF as a document post. On Facebook, use the slide images.

Will it sound like my business?

Boomp reads your public pages, so it starts from your own services, details, and words. Your website is a starting point, not complete knowledge of your business. Check every slide and claim before you post it.
